Good to know

Before you choose

  • What the price includes. Menu design, ingredients, chef, kitchen team and servers for your event.
  • What is added. 20% service charge and Florida sales tax. Rentals and venue fees are quoted for your event.
  • Allergies. Allergens are listed under each dish. Tell us about allergies when you inquire and we plan safe plates for those guests.
  • Dietary needs. Vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free plates are made to order at no extra cost.
  • Seasonality. Dishes follow the season. When an ingredient is out of season, we offer an equal replacement.
  • Tasting and guest count. Complimentary tasting for two for weddings booked this season. Final count is due 14 days before the event.
