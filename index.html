Questions

How much does it cost?

Each menu has one price per guest: Classic $125, Garden Grill (BBQ) $130, Signature $150, Premium $175 and Wedding $200. The kids menu is $50 per child, and a private chef at home is $150, $200 or $250 per guest plus groceries. Florida sales tax is added. See our menus for details.

How far ahead should we book?

We can plan a wedding with as little as one month’s notice, and smaller private events even sooner. Weekends from November to April fill up first, so ask about your date as early as you can.

What does the price include?

Everything on your menu: ingredients we choose ourselves, cooking in our licensed kitchen, delivery, and finishing on site. Sauces, desserts and breads are made in house. Staff, rentals and additional services are quoted separately, and Florida sales tax is added. Example: the Classic menu for 50 guests is from $6,250 plus tax.

Is there a minimum guest count?

Yes, 10 guests for our catering menus. The private chef dinner starts from 4 guests.

How long is the service?

Our menus are planned for an event of about 4 hours. Tell us if yours is longer and we will plan the quantities and timing.

Are there travel fees, or extra charges for a yacht?

No. Delivery within Miami and the surrounding areas is included, and there is no extra charge for delivering to a yacht.

Do you provide drinks or a bartender?

We do not serve alcohol. We can introduce you to a licensed bartending partner, booked and paid directly with them.

Can you cater for allergies and dietary needs?

Yes. Allergens are listed under every dish, and vegetarian, vegan and gluten-free plates are made to order at no extra cost. Tell us when you inquire and we plan the menu around your guests.

Are you licensed and insured?

Yes. ELART Catering & Events LLC is a licensed Florida caterer, and every menu is cooked in a licensed commercial kitchen. We carry $1M general liability insurance and send a certificate of insurance to your venue on request. Our chef is a ServSafe Certified Food Protection Manager.

Do you work at any venue?

Yes. We cater venues, private homes, estates and yachts. Proudly serving Miami, Aventura, Sunny Isles Beach, Hallandale Beach, Hollywood and surrounding communities within approximately 30 miles. Tell us where your event will be and we will plan the rest.
