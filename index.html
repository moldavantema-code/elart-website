Questions

How much does it cost?

Our menus have one fixed price per guest: $150 for a plated wedding dinner, $195 for the full evening with the larger dessert bar, $150 plus groceries for a chef’s table at home, and $125 for a family-style dinner for relaxed gatherings. Florida sales tax is added. See the full menu for what each includes.

How far ahead should we book?

Weddings 6–12 months ahead, private events 3–6 weeks. Ask about your date even if it is sooner.

What does the price include?

The menu, cooked in our kitchen and delivered to your event. Florida sales tax is added. Rentals and venue fees are quoted for your event.

Allergies and dietary needs?

Yes. Tell us when you inquire and we plan the menu around them.

Do you work at any venue?

Venues, private homes and yachts across Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach. Tell us your venue and we will confirm.
