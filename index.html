Questions

How much does it cost?

Weddings and celebrations start at $150 per guest for food and service, with a $7,500 minimum. A chef’s table at home starts at $150 per guest, with a $1,500 minimum. A 20% service charge and Florida sales tax are added to every quote. Share your guest count and we will suggest the right format.

How far ahead should we book?

Weddings 6–12 months ahead, private events 3–6 weeks. Ask about your date even if it is sooner.

Is the tasting included?

For weddings booked in our first season, the tasting for two is complimentary.

Allergies and dietary needs?

Yes. Tell us when you inquire and we plan the menu around them.

Do you work at any venue?

Venues, private homes and yachts across Miami-Dade, Broward and Palm Beach. Tell us your venue and we will confirm.
