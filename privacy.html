Privacy policy

How ELART Catering & Events uses the information you send us through this website.

  • Who we are. ELART Catering & Events, a licensed caterer in Miami, Florida. Questions about this policy: events@elartcatering.com or +1 (754) 777-1169.
  • What we collect. Only what you enter in our forms: your name, email, phone number, event date, guest count, venue or marina, budget and any notes you add. Partner applications also include your company name, category and website.
  • How we use it. To reply to your inquiry, prepare a quote, plan your menu and event, and stay in touch about your booking.
  • Text messages. If you check the box on a form, we may text you about your inquiry and event. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to opt out at any time, or HELP for help. Consent to receive texts is not a condition of booking.
  • Sharing. We do not sell or rent your information. Mobile numbers and text-message consent are never shared with third parties or affiliates for marketing. Form submissions are stored by our website host (Netlify) so we can receive them. We share details with a venue or partner only when you ask us to, or when the law requires it.
  • Cookies. This site does not use advertising cookies. Our host may record basic technical data, such as IP address and browser type, to keep the site secure and working.
  • How long we keep it. As long as we need it to answer you, plan your event and keep normal business records.
  • Your choices. Email us to see, correct or delete the information you sent us.
  • Changes. If we update this policy, we will change the date at the top of this page.
