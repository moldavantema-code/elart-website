Artem’s story

Creating my own table

For most of my career, I was focused on building kitchens, developing menus, and opening new cafés. I worked my way up as a chef, learned pastry, managed teams, and helped turn ideas into successful businesses.

I genuinely enjoyed that work. But at some point, I started asking myself a question:

Why am I putting so much energy into building someone else's dream instead of creating my own?

This year, I decided it was time to change that.

I started working as a private chef for families in Miami. I eventually spent a long time with one family, and looking back, I can say it was one of the best experiences of my career.

It challenged me in ways I hadn't expected.

In a restaurant, you create a menu, perfect the recipes, and serve them every day. But when you're cooking for the same family, things are different.

I couldn't just keep serving the same dishes. I had to come up with something new almost every day. Different flavors, different presentations, sometimes completely new ideas.

Some days I would take a traditional dish and give it a modern twist. Other days I would experiment with ingredients or rethink the way something was served. Not everything worked on the first try, but that was part of the experience.

And I loved it.

It pushed me to become more creative, more flexible, and more attentive to the people I was cooking for.

Great cooking isn't always about impressing someone. Sometimes it's simply about understanding what they enjoy and finding a new way to make them happy.

I'm grateful for that experience because it changed the way I think about food.

Today, I'm taking everything I've learned and putting it into ELART. Together with Olena, we're building a catering company where we can bring that same personal approach to weddings, private dinners, and celebrations.

I don't want people to choose ELART just because our food looks beautiful. I want them to feel that we listened, understood what they wanted, and created something specifically for them.

That's the kind of company I want to build.

Olena’s story

My name is Elena Dinov. My career in the food industry began with owning and managing restaurants in Boston and Manhattan, New York, followed by two organic and healthy meal delivery companies in Brooklyn.

In 2021, I moved to Miami, where I continued to develop my approach to healthy eating. I created personalized menus for clients pursuing weight loss, focusing on balanced nutrition, thoughtful food combinations, and calorie management. Seeing their results was deeply rewarding. The next major step in my career was launching my own catering company.

Through my team’s professionalism, the quality of our food, and our attentive approach to client service, the business achieved significant success and was recognized among Florida’s best businesses in both 2023 and 2024.

I am excited to bring my business together with Artem’s and take our shared catering venture to the next level. This decision is grounded in mutual trust, respect, and a shared commitment to excellence, as well as our business goals. We both value expertise, creativity, reliability, and a thoughtful, responsible approach to every event and every client. Joining forces allows us to bring together our distinct perspectives and complementary strengths.

My experience in restaurant and catering management, operations, and client service is complemented by Artem’s expertise in culinary arts, pastry, and innovative menu development.

Together, we believe we can offer an even higher standard of service, expand our menu, elevate the planning and execution of every event, and build a company defined by consistent growth and professionalism.
