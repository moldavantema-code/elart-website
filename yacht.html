Before you charter

Does the boat need a kitchen?

No. Everything is cooked in our kitchen and arrives ready to serve. A fridge or cooler on board helps keep cold dishes fresh.

How much does it cost?

We cater charters with our regular menus, one price per guest: Classic $125, Signature $150, Premium $175 and Wedding $200. The kids menu is $50 per child. Florida sales tax is added.

How far ahead should we book?

3–7 days ahead. During the Miami Boat Show, Art Basel and holiday weekends, book earlier.

Can you serve sushi, oysters or a raw bar?

Yes, when the boat can keep them cold for the whole trip. We check cold storage with the captain first.

Do you work with our captain and crew?

Yes. We confirm delivery time, cold storage and the route with the captain before the charter.

What if the weather cancels the charter?

If the weather cancels the charter, contact us as soon as possible and we will work with you on a new date.

Do you provide drinks?

Non-alcoholic drinks, like fresh juices and cold brew, can be added to your menu. We do not serve alcohol; it is provided by the charter or the guests.
