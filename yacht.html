Before you charter

Does the boat need a galley?

For a chef on board, yes: a working stove, refrigeration and fresh water. Without a galley we recommend dockside delivery, with food that needs no cooking on board.

How much does it cost?

Each yacht menu has one price per guest: brunch $75, sandbar lunch $85, sunset cocktails $90, delivered to your dock. A chef on board with the tasting menu is $195 per guest plus groceries. A 20% service charge and Florida sales tax are added. A bartender is $55 per hour.

How far ahead should we book?

3–7 days for delivery, 1–2 weeks for a chef on board. During the Miami Boat Show, Art Basel and holiday weekends, book earlier.

Can you serve sushi, oysters or a raw bar?

Yes, when the boat can keep them cold for the whole trip. We check cold storage with the captain first.

Do you work with our captain and crew?

Yes. We confirm boarding time, galley space and the route with the captain before the charter.
