Good to know

Before you book

  • What the price includes. The menu, cooked in a licensed commercial kitchen and delivered to your event.
  • What is added. Florida sales tax. Staff, rentals and additional services are quoted separately.
  • Timing. Menus are planned for an event of about 4 hours.
  • Delivery. No travel fees within Miami and the surrounding areas, including delivery to a yacht.
  • Drinks. We do not serve alcohol. We can introduce you to a licensed bartending partner.
  • Allergies. Allergens are listed under each dish. Tell us about allergies when you inquire and we plan safe plates for those guests.
